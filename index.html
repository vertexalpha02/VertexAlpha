The VertexAlpha Framework

A structured way to study markets instead of blindly following buy/sell calls.

01 — STRUCTURE

Market Structure Mapping

Identify important highs, lows, trends and market zones before considering any trade idea.

02 — LIQUIDITY

Liquidity Behaviour

Study areas where price may react and understand why volatility increases around important levels.

03 — PROBABILITY

Probability Filter

Every setup is evaluated through risk, reward, confirmation and invalidation rather than emotion.

04 — RISK

Risk First Model

The objective is not to predict every move. The objective is to control downside when the market behaves differently than expected.

05 — TIMING

Multi-Timeframe Context

Higher timeframe context is combined with lower timeframe confirmation to reduce impulsive decisions.

06 — DISCIPLINE

Execution Discipline

A good framework is useful only when the trader follows predefined rules consistently.
